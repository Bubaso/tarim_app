STORY HIGHLIGHTS

- The Turquoise Nexus Initiative aims to address fragmentation in the way
  national climate commitments reflect water, agriculture, food security, and
  adaptation measures.
- TNI is a proposed programme of FAO's FAST Partnership that will "bring together
  governments, international organizations, research institutions, civil society,
  and the private sector around a common agenda for climate-resilient,
  water-centered agriculture and food systems".
- It will aim to support developing countries in integrating food-water-climate
  approaches into their NDCs and implementation efforts under the Paris Agreement
  on climate change.

GÖVDE (ENB'den ayrışan kısımlar)

"At a side event held during the 2026 Bonn Climate Change Conference, the Food
and Agriculture Organization of the UN (FAO) and Türkiye's Presidency of the 2026
UN Climate Change Conference (UNFCCC COP 31) presented an initiative to create a
global mechanism to integrate food systems transformation, sustainable water
management, and climate adaptation."

"Umay Gökçe Özkan … said TNI is designed to address fragmentation through policy
coherence, implementation support, knowledge generation, technical exchange,
investment readiness, and finance alignment."

"The side event took place in Bonn, Germany, on 16 June 2026."
