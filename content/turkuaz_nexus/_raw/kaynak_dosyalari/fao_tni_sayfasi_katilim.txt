ÇEKİM : 2026-11-12
NOT   : Sayfanın iki katlanır bölümü ("How TNI works", "Who can participate")
        JS ile açılıyor; metin aşağıda.

TANIM (sayfadan)
  "The Turquoise Nexus Initiative (TNI) is a country-led, multi-stakeholder
   initiative proposed as a flagship programme of FAO's Food and Agriculture
   for Sustainable Transformation (FAST) Partnership. The Initiative will be led
   by the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry of Türkiye under the coordination
   of the COP31 Presidency."

GEREKÇE (sayfadan — SAYFADA KULLANILACAK RAKAM BURADA)
  "Agriculture accounts for 70 percent of global freshwater withdrawals, yet the
   food–water–climate nexus remains underrepresented in national climate
   commitments."

TÜRKİYE'NİN MODELİ — DÖRT UNSUR (sayfadan birebir)
  "Drawing on Türkiye's experience in water-centred agricultural transformation,
   including large-scale irrigation modernization, national drought management
   and early warning systems, drought-resistant and water-efficient crop
   development programmes, and the integration of water management into national
   food policy…"

"WHO CAN PARTICIPATE" — TAM METİN

  "Early expressions of interest following the Initiative's presentation during
   the UNFCCC June Climate Meetings (SB64) highlight significant interest from
   countries, technical institutions, civil society organizations, research
   institutions and finance partners in engaging through policy coherence,
   knowledge exchange, implementation support and investment alignment. The
   online expression of interest form is open. Countries and organizations
   interested in participating are encouraged to complete the form to register
   their interest: https://forms.gle/27kGfnQ4LMVktiMg8
   As a proposed flagship programme of the FAST Partnership, TNI will operate
   within the Partnership's existing institutional framework. FAST Partnership
   Members, Observers and Technical Partners, including governments,
   international organizations, research institutions, civil society and the
   private sector, will be encouraged to contribute according to their expertise
   through knowledge sharing, technical cooperation and advocacy support.
   Countries and organizations interested in participating in the Turquoise
   Nexus Initiative that are not yet part of the FAST Partnership will be able
   to engage through the Partnership's existing participation modalities,
   including as Members, Observers or Technical Partners, as appropriate."
