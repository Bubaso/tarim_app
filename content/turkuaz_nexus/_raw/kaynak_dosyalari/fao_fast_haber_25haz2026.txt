ALINTILAR (sayfadan birebir)

Fatma Varank, Çevre, Şehircilik ve İklim Değişikliği Bakan Yardımcısı:
  "Our goal as the COP31 Chair is to move the global climate agenda from
   rhetoric to action. The Turquoise Nexus Initiative is one concrete example
   of this approach."

Dr Ahmet Bağcı, Tarım ve Orman Bakan Yardımcısı:
  "Our message is simple: food, water and climate are not separate issues,
   they are closely connected and investment strategies should reflect this
   reality."
  "The Turkish experience can offer valuable lessons for regions facing
   similar issues."

Kaveh Zahedi, FAO Genel Direktör Yardımcısı:
  "There is growing momentum to address climate change through the window of
   agriculture and food. The FAST Partnership serves as the primary COP-to-COP
   mechanism for this. Its flagship programmes leave a legacy beyond COP,
   binding together policy and implementation and ensuring that food security,
   resilient and sustainable agriculture, and climate finance continue to
   remain high priorities on the international climate agenda."

Martial Bernoux, FAO Kıdemli Doğal Kaynaklar Görevlisi:
  "We are pleased to welcome TNI under the FAST Partnership umbrella. No single
   initiative can address the multiple crises we face, including climate change,
   biodiversity loss, food insecurity, and water scarcity. The additional focus
   on water that TNI brings is a vital dimension in agriculture."

ÜÇ SÜTUN (sayfadan birebir — FAO TNI sayfasındaki metinle aynı)

• Policy coherence and implementation support to strengthen alignment across
  food, water and climate policy frameworks;
• Knowledge generation and technical exchange to promote learning, innovation
  and the sharing of best practices, including through case studies and a
  global knowledge network linking research, extension and policy institutions;
• Investment readiness and finance alignment to help countries mobilize and
  direct resources towards integrated solutions, including the development of a
  pipeline of bankable projects that connect water-centred agriculture
  priorities with climate finance opportunities.

ÖZKAN'IN TEŞHİSİ (sayfadan)
  "national food strategies, water frameworks and climate commitments remain
   structurally misaligned, with the governance gap most acute in semi-arid and
   water-stressed regions across MENA, Sub-Saharan Africa and Central Asia."

KAPANIŞ CÜMLESİ (25 Haziran itibarıyla)
  "The formal launch of the Turquoise Nexus Initiative is expected to take
   place at COP31 in Türkiye."
