I, Grant Kari, Acting General Manager – Water Branch, Australian Competition and Consumer 
Commission (ACCC), state that: 
1. An ACCC representative from the Water Branch observed the administration of the trade 
randomisation process conducted by the Victorian Department of Energy, Environment

representative.

randomisation tool during the randomisation process.

Opening Report – July 2026 matches the output of the randomisation tool.
