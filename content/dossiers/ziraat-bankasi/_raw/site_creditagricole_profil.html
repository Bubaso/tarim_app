Working every day in the interest of our customers and society

Credit Agricole Group in few points:

retail customers

12.3 million

mutual shareholders

160,000

employees worldwide

 

 

Olivier Gavalda, Chief Executive Officer of Crédit Agricole S.A.

 

We are a retail bank because we continue to rely on the strength of our local network, but also because we adapt to changes in customer trends. We provide straightforward, independent advice, offering both the best digital services and the expertise of your own personal advisor.

 

We work hard every day to create a new banking experience that is both 100% digital and 100% personal.

Each of our customers can choose their preferred means of contact with their advisor, evaluate their needs or monitor their transactions at any time, whether online, on smartphones and tablets, by phone or in branch.

 

We have always fostered an environment of transparency and trust, just for you.

Transparency, closeness to our customers, accountability and openness: these are the values that have guided us for more than 120 years.

Our business lines put us right at the heart of the lives and projects of our customers and of society as a whole, which bestows upon us great responsibility. We make sure the value we create is shared fairly and sustainably with our customers, members, shareholders, employees and partners
