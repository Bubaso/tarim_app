# 29 · Glossary

## A

**Accuracy** (*Doğruluk*). In a classification model, the number of correct predictions divided by the total number of predictions. *Source: Google, Machine Learning Glossary.* · Appears in A short history; Computer vision and targeted spraying; Post-harvest; Livestock; Forestry; Advisory services and language models; Satellites and remote sensing; Risks and limits; Failures and overstatements; Stories from the field.

**Agricultural extension** (*Tarımsal yayım*). An umbrella term for all the activities that provide the information and advisory services needed by farmers and other actors in agrifood systems. *Source: FAO (Christoplos, 2010).* · Appears in Where things stand today; Computer vision and targeted spraying; Advisory services and language models; Risks and limits; Stories from the field.

**Artificial intelligence (AI)** (*Yapay zekâ*). In the EU AI Act's definition, an AI system is a machine-based system designed to operate with varying levels of autonomy that infers from the input it receives how to generate outputs such as predictions, content, recommendations or decisions. *Source: EU AI Act (2024).* · Appears in Where things stand today; Terms; A short history; Soil and land; Seed, breeding and genetics; Sowing and variable-rate application; Irrigation and water; Crop monitoring and yield forecasting; Harvest and robotics; Post-harvest; Livestock; Greenhouse and controlled environment; Aquaculture and fisheries; Forestry; Markets, prices and supply chain; Climate, risk and crop insurance; Advisory services and language models; Satellites and remote sensing; Sensors and agricultural IoT; Data; The world, region by region; Türkiye today; Regulation and policy; Türkiye directory; Risks and limits; Failures and overstatements.

**Autonomous mobile machinery** (*Otonom mobil makine*). In the EU Machinery Regulation's definition, mobile machinery with an autonomous mode, in which all its essential safety functions are ensured in its travel and working area without permanent interaction of an operator. *Source: EU Machinery Regulation (2023).* · Appears in Harvest and robotics; Türkiye today; Regulation and policy; Risks and limits; Failures and overstatements; Stories from the field.

**Auto-steer (automated guidance)** (*Otomatik dümenleme (otomatik yönlendirme)*). Satellite-positioning technology that steers self-propelled farm machines such as tractors, combine harvesters and sprayers automatically. It reduces overlaps and skips in the field. *Source: FAO, The State of Food and Agriculture 2022, glossary; USDA Economic Research Service (2023).* · Appears in Where things stand today; Terms; The world, region by region.

## B

**Basis risk** (*Baz riski*). The risk in index insurance that arises when the index measurements do not match an insured person's actual losses. *Source: World Bank Group, Global Index Insurance Facility (GIIF).* · Appears in Climate, risk and crop insurance.

**Big data** (*Büyük veri*). Large, diverse, complex data sets generated from instruments, sensors, financial transactions, social media and other digital means, typically beyond the storage capacity and processing power of personal computers and basic analytical software. *Source: FAO, The State of Food and Agriculture 2022, glossary.* · Appears in Terms; Data.

**Blockchain** (*Blokzincir*). A tamper-evident and tamper-resistant digital ledger implemented in a distributed fashion, without a central repository and usually without a central authority. *Source: NIST (2018).* · Appears in Markets, prices and supply chain.

## C

**Chatbot** (*Sohbet robotu*). An application of a large language model: the user holds a back-and-forth dialogue with a machine learning system, typically a large language model, and earlier questions and answers become the context for what follows. *Source: Google, Machine Learning Glossary.* · Appears in A short history; Advisory services and language models; The world, region by region.

**Classification model** (*Sınıflandırma modeli*). A model whose prediction is a class; for example, a model that predicts a tree's species (maple, oak or baobab?). *Source: Google, Machine Learning Glossary.* · Appears in Crop monitoring and yield forecasting; Livestock; Aquaculture and fisheries; Forestry; Türkiye today; Risks and limits.

**Climate-smart agriculture** (*İklim akıllı tarım*). An approach defined and presented by FAO in 2010. It has three pillars: sustainably increasing agricultural productivity and incomes, adapting and building resilience to climate change, and reducing or removing greenhouse gas emissions where possible. It is not a single technology or practice that can be applied everywhere. *Source: FAO, Climate-Smart Agriculture Sourcebook (2013).* · Appears in Where things stand today; Terms; Failures and overstatements.

**Computer vision (image processing)** (*Görüntü işleme (bilgisayarlı görü)*). A field of AI and machine learning that enables computers to interpret, analyse and understand visual information (images and videos) to perform tasks such as object recognition, facial recognition and scene understanding. *Source: UK Government, Artificial Intelligence Playbook (2025).* · Appears in A short history; Computer vision and targeted spraying; Post-harvest; Greenhouse and controlled environment; Türkiye today.

**Controlled environment agriculture** (*Kontrollü ortam tarımı*). Agriculture that includes greenhouse farming and vertical farming; greenhouses are its most common form. *Source: FAO, The State of Food and Agriculture 2022.* · Appears in Greenhouse and controlled environment.

## D

**Data space** (*Veri alanı*). In the Türkiye AI Action Plan's definition, a structured environment in which companies and public bodies share their data in a controlled way under common rules, secure work rooms and standard licences. Under the EU Data Act, common European data spaces are sector-specific or cross-sectoral frameworks of common standards and practices for sharing or jointly processing data. *Source: Türkiye Artificial Intelligence Action Plan (2026-2030), glossary; EU Data Act (2023).* · Appears in Data.

**Decision support system** (*Karar destek sistemi*). A computer program designed explicitly to help people make better decisions. *Source: Heilman and colleagues (2005).* · Appears in Where things stand today; Irrigation and water; Forestry; Satellites and remote sensing; Türkiye today.

**Deep learning** (*Derin öğrenme*). A subset of machine learning that uses more complex model structures, including sophisticated neural networks, to learn complex patterns and representations from large amounts of data. *Source: UK Government, Artificial Intelligence Playbook (2025).* · Appears in Where things stand today; A short history; Seed, breeding and genetics; Harvest and robotics; Aquaculture and fisheries; Forestry; Markets, prices and supply chain; Advisory services and language models; Satellites and remote sensing; Türkiye today; Risks and limits; Stories from the field.

**Digital farming** (*Dijital tarım*). The diffusion of information technologies such as the internet, the Internet of Things, mobile devices and predictive analytics to enhance the collection, exchange, combination, analysis and access of digital content in agriculture. *Source: USDA Economic Research Service (2023).* · Appears in Terms; Data; The world, region by region; Türkiye today; Risks and limits; Stories from the field.

**Drone (uncrewed aerial vehicle)** (*İnsansız hava aracı (İHA, dron)*). A flying autonomous machine that can be guided by remote control or by a software-controlled device. In agriculture it is often used to collect aerial images or to apply fertiliser, seed, pesticides or other inputs. *Source: FAO, The State of Food and Agriculture 2022, glossary.* · Appears in Where things stand today; Computer vision and targeted spraying; Forestry; Climate, risk and crop insurance; Satellites and remote sensing; The world, region by region; Türkiye today; Stories from the field.

## E

**Early warning system** (*Erken uyarı sistemi*). An integrated system of hazard monitoring and forecasting, disaster risk assessment, communication and preparedness that enables individuals, communities, governments and businesses to take timely action. *Source: UN General Assembly, report on disaster risk terminology (2016).* · Appears in Crop monitoring and yield forecasting; Forestry; Markets, prices and supply chain; Climate, risk and crop insurance; Türkiye today.

**Electronic ear tag (electronic identification)** (*Elektronik küpe (elektronik tanımlama)*). The use of a microchip or electronic transponder embedded in a tag, bolus or implant to identify an individual farm animal. *Source: FAO, The State of Food and Agriculture 2022, glossary.* · Appears in Livestock; The world, region by region.

**Expert system** (*Uzman sistem*). A computer program that can handle problems in a limited, strictly defined domain at an expert level, using expert knowledge and reasoning processes. Its components are a knowledge base (rules and data), an inference engine and a user interface. *Source: Schreinemakers and colleagues (1988).* · Appears in A short history; Risks and limits.

## F

**F-1 score** (*F-1 skoru*). A classification metric that relies on both precision and recall, rolling them up into a single number. *Source: Google, Machine Learning Glossary.* · Appears in Risks and limits.

**Fine-tuning** (*İnce ayar*). A second, task-specific training pass on a pre-trained model to refine its parameters for a specific use. *Source: Google, Machine Learning Glossary.* · Appears in Advisory services and language models.

**Foundation model** (*Temel model*). A very large pre-trained model trained on an enormous and diverse training set. *Source: Google, Machine Learning Glossary.* · Appears in Türkiye today.

## G

**Genomic selection** (*Genomik seçilim*). Basing selection decisions on genomic breeding values, which are calculated as the sum of the effects of dense genetic markers across the entire genome. *Source: Hayes and colleagues (2009).* · Appears in Seed, breeding and genetics; Livestock.

**GNSS and GPS (satellite positioning)** (*GNSS ve GPS (uydu konumlama)*). Any system that uses satellite signals to provide location information is a global navigation satellite system (GNSS). GPS is the US system; because it was the first available for civilian use, its name is sometimes used for all of them. *Source: FAO, The State of Food and Agriculture 2022, glossary.* · Appears in Terms; A short history; Sowing and variable-rate application; Aquaculture and fisheries; The world, region by region; Stories from the field.

## I

**Index insurance** (*Endeks sigortası*). An approach to insurance that pays out benefits for loss of assets and investments resulting from weather and catastrophic events on the basis of a predetermined index (for example rainfall level). *Source: World Bank Group, Global Index Insurance Facility (GIIF).* · Appears in Climate, risk and crop insurance.

**Internet of Things (IoT)** (*Nesnelerin interneti (IoT)*). A system in which devices, including mobile phones, sensors, drones, machines and satellites, are connected to the internet. *Source: FAO, The State of Food and Agriculture 2022, glossary.* · Appears in Terms; Sensors and agricultural IoT.

## L

**Large language model** (*Büyük dil modeli*). An AI model trained on large amounts of text data that can generate, understand and translate natural-language text. *Source: Türkiye Artificial Intelligence Action Plan (2026-2030), glossary.* · Appears in Advisory services and language models.

**LoRaWAN** (*LoRaWAN*). A low-power, wide-area (LPWA) networking specification designed to connect battery-operated devices wirelessly to the internet in regional, national or global networks. *Source: LoRa Alliance.* · Appears in Sensors and agricultural IoT.

## M

**Machine learning** (*Makine öğrenmesi*). A program or system that trains a model from input data; the trained model can make useful predictions from new data drawn from the same distribution as the data used to train it. *Source: Google, Machine Learning Glossary.* · Appears in Where things stand today; Terms; A short history; Soil and land; Seed, breeding and genetics; Sowing and variable-rate application; Crop monitoring and yield forecasting; Aquaculture and fisheries; Forestry; Markets, prices and supply chain; Climate, risk and crop insurance; Advisory services and language models; The world, region by region; Türkiye today; Regulation and policy; Risks and limits.

**Multispectral imagery** (*Multispektral (çok bantlı) görüntü*). Imagery from a scanner that collects data over a variety of different wavelength ranges, the most commonly used scanning system. Each Sentinel-2 satellite, for example, carries a multispectral optical instrument that samples 13 spectral bands. *Source: Natural Resources Canada (NRCan); Copernicus SentiWiki (ESA).* · Appears in Satellites and remote sensing; Türkiye today; Stories from the field.

## N

**Natural language processing** (*Doğal dil işleme*). A field within AI that focuses on processing human language: teaching computers to process what a user said or typed using linguistic rules. *Source: UK Government, Artificial Intelligence Playbook (2025); Google, Machine Learning Glossary.* · Appears in Advisory services and language models.

**NDVI** (*NDVI*). An index that quantifies vegetation greenness, calculated as a ratio between red and near-infrared values. It is used to understand vegetation density and assess changes in plant health. *Source: US Geological Survey (USGS).* · Appears in Seed, breeding and genetics; Crop monitoring and yield forecasting.

## O

**Object recognition** (*Nesne tanıma*). Detecting the presence and location of multiple classes of objects within images or videos; for example, locating dogs in an image. *Source: Google AI Edge, MediaPipe Object Detector.* · Appears in Harvest and robotics.

**Open data** (*Açık veri*). In the Open Definition, which defines open data, open content and open knowledge together: data that anyone is free to access, use, modify and share, subject at most to measures that preserve provenance and openness. *Source: Open Knowledge Foundation, Open Definition.* · Appears in Satellites and remote sensing; Data.

## P

**Phenotyping** (*Fenotipleme*). A quantitative description of the plant's anatomical, ontogenetic, physiological and biochemical properties. *Source: Walter and colleagues (2015).* · Appears in Seed, breeding and genetics.

**Precision** (*Kesinlik*). A metric for classification models: when the model predicted the positive class, the percentage of those predictions that were correct. *Source: Google, Machine Learning Glossary.* · Appears in Advisory services and language models.

**Precision farming** (*Hassas tarım*). A management strategy that gathers, processes and analyses temporal, spatial and individual data and combines them with other information, to manage variation in the field accurately and to support management decisions. *Source: FAO, The State of Food and Agriculture 2022, glossary.* · Appears in Where things stand today; Terms; A short history; Sowing and variable-rate application; The world, region by region; Türkiye directory; Risks and limits.

**Precision livestock farming** (*Hassas hayvancılık*). A data-based livestock management strategy that monitors and controls individual animal or group productivity, environment, health and welfare continuously, in real time and automatically. *Source: FAO, The State of Food and Agriculture 2022, glossary.* · Appears in Livestock.

## R

**Random forest** (*Rastgele orman*). An ensemble of decision trees, each trained with a specific random noise. *Source: Google, Machine Learning Glossary.* · Appears in Sowing and variable-rate application; Crop monitoring and yield forecasting; Forestry.

**Ransomware** (*Fidye yazılımı*). Malware designed to encrypt files on a device, rendering the files and the systems that rely on them unusable; malicious actors then demand a ransom for decryption. *Source: US Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA).* · Appears in Risks and limits.

**Recall** (*Duyarlılık*). A metric for classification models: when the ground truth was the positive class, the percentage of cases the model correctly identified as positive. *Source: Google, Machine Learning Glossary.* · Appears in Advisory services and language models.

**Remote sensing** (*Uzaktan algılama*). Detecting and monitoring the physical characteristics of an area by measuring its reflected and emitted radiation at a distance, typically from a satellite or aircraft. *Source: US Geological Survey (USGS).* · Appears in Terms; A short history; Irrigation and water; Crop monitoring and yield forecasting; Climate, risk and crop insurance; Satellites and remote sensing; Türkiye today; Risks and limits.

**Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)** (*Belgeye dayalı yanıt üretme (RAG)*). A technique that improves the output of a large language model by grounding it in sources of knowledge retrieved after the model was trained. *Source: Google, Machine Learning Glossary.* · Appears in Advisory services and language models.

**Robot** (*Robot*). A machine capable of autonomous operation without direct human intervention. *Source: FAO, The State of Food and Agriculture 2022, glossary.* · Appears in Where things stand today; Terms; Sowing and variable-rate application; Harvest and robotics; Livestock; Greenhouse and controlled environment; The world, region by region; Türkiye today; Türkiye directory; Risks and limits; Failures and overstatements.

## S

**Sensor** (*Sensör*). A device that produces a voltage or current output representative of some physical property being measured (such as speed, temperature or flow). *Source: NIST, CSRC glossary.* · Appears in Where things stand today; Terms; A short history; Seed, breeding and genetics; Sowing and variable-rate application; Irrigation and water; Post-harvest; Livestock; Greenhouse and controlled environment; Aquaculture and fisheries; Climate, risk and crop insurance; Sensors and agricultural IoT; Data; Risks and limits; Failures and overstatements; Stories from the field.

**Smart farming** (*Akıllı tarım*). A development that emphasises the use of information and communication technology in the cyber-physical farm management cycle. *Source: Wolfert and colleagues (2017).* · Appears in Terms; A short history; Sowing and variable-rate application; Sensors and agricultural IoT; The world, region by region; Türkiye today; Türkiye directory; Risks and limits.

**Soil moisture sensor** (*Toprak nem sensörü*). A sensor that, when used in irrigation scheduling, readily provides timely and precise information on the water in the soil and the soil's water-holding potential. *Source: Akın and Ünlenen (2017).* · Appears in Where things stand today; Irrigation and water; Stories from the field.

**Support vector machine** (*Destek vektör makinesi*). A classification algorithm that seeks to maximise the margin between positive and negative classes by mapping input data to a higher-dimensional space. *Source: Google, Machine Learning Glossary.* · Appears in Post-harvest.

**Synthetic aperture radar (SAR)** (*Sentetik açıklıklı radar (SAR)*). A type of active data collection in which an instrument sends out a pulse of energy and records the amount reflected back after it interacts with the Earth. It can create high-resolution imagery night or day, regardless of weather conditions. *Source: NASA Earthdata.* · Appears in Forestry; Climate, risk and crop insurance; Satellites and remote sensing.

## T

**Traceability** (*İzlenebilirlik*). The ability to follow the movement of a food through specified stages of production, processing and distribution. *Source: Codex Alimentarius (2006).* · Appears in Markets, prices and supply chain.

**Training data** (*Eğitim verisi*). The subset of a dataset used to train a model. *Source: Google, Machine Learning Glossary.* · Appears in Terms; Seed, breeding and genetics; Computer vision and targeted spraying.

## V

**Variable-rate application** (*Değişken oranlı uygulama*). Applying seed, lime, fertiliser or pesticides at custom rates that can change over small distances; the equipment that does this is known as variable rate technology. *Source: USDA Economic Research Service (2023).* · Appears in Terms; A short history; Soil and land; Sowing and variable-rate application; Satellites and remote sensing; The world, region by region.

**Vertical farming** (*Dikey tarım*). Indoor farming in a completely controlled environment, growing crops vertically year-round. *Source: FAO, The State of Food and Agriculture 2022, glossary.* · Appears in Greenhouse and controlled environment; Failures and overstatements.

**Virtual fencing** (*Sanal çit*). A technology that equips animals with satellite positioning transponders to determine their location and uses audio alerts, electric shocks or other prompts to keep them within set boundaries. *Source: FAO, The State of Food and Agriculture 2022, glossary.* · Appears in Stories from the field.

## Y

**Yield map** (*Verim haritası*). A map that shows crop yields and the variation in yields within and across fields. *Source: USDA Economic Research Service (2023).* · Appears in Data; The world, region by region.

## Z

**Zero-shot prompting** (*Örneksiz istem (zero-shot)*). A prompt that gives the large language model no example of how it should respond. *Source: Google, Machine Learning Glossary.* · Appears in Advisory services and language models.
