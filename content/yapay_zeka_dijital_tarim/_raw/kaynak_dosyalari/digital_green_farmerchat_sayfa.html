The Challenge

Millions of farmers make critical decisions every day without timely advice

Every season, smallholder farmers face decisions with lasting consequences: when to plant, what to apply, how to protect a crop. The right guidance at the right moment can decide a year’s yield and their family’s livelihood.

FarmerChat meets farmers where they are: in their language, on their phone, in seconds. No appointments, no jargon, no waiting for answers.
