Türkiye directory: start-ups, universities, public projects

https://tarimportali.net/yapay-zeka-dijital-tarim/turkiye-dizini

In Türkiye, work on AI and digital technology in agriculture is under way in four places: in the systems of public bodies, in the state's research and support programmes, in universities and in start-ups. In the public sector, AI is mostly used, or being prepared for use, in monitoring: identifying crops, idle land and orchards from satellites, decision support in forest fires, tracking fishing vessels. In the 10 projects accepted in smart agriculture, food and livestock under TÜBİTAK's 1711 AI Ecosystem Call, companies work together with universities and research institutes. On the start-up side, a 2025 report by FAO and the EBRD counts about 35 agricultural technology start-ups in Türkiye and finds this number relatively low for the size of the country and its farm sector.

Research and support programmes

TÜBİTAK's 1711 AI Ecosystem Call aims to turn AI technologies into products or solutions that meet the needs of client organisations. The first call opened in 2022 and a call opens every year; one of the programme's five priority areas is smart agriculture, food and livestock. So far the programme has supported 64 projects with 325,089,753 lira; this total covers all five areas. These are projects that were found worthy of support. According to the Plant Protection Central Research Institute, one of its partners, the greenhouse robot project started on 1 January 2026.

TÜBİTAK BİLGEM's AI Institute has a Smart Agriculture, Food and Livestock competence centre. Its fields of work include estimating production from satellite images and soil analysis results, and raising meat and milk output with continuous measurements taken from animals. The centre's page also mentions cooperation with the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry's general directorates of Food and Control and of Livestock.

The Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry, according to the minister's statement of 4 June 2026, has set up an Artificial Intelligence and Digital Agriculture Technologies Research Centre.

The Türkiye Climate Smart and Competitive Agricultural Growth Project (TUCSAP) is financed by the World Bank; according to the ministry's 2025 activity report, grant programmes under the project had not yet started.

The Digital Villages Initiative is a technical cooperation project of TAGEM and FAO: it started in 2023, was completed in December 2025 and covered Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Georgia and Türkiye.

TEKNOFEST's Agricultural Technologies Competition has awarded places every year since 2020; high school and university students, graduates, start-ups and private companies can take part. The Ankara Development Agency's TAGTech Programme, according to the FAO–EBRD report, provides farmers and agricultural technology start-ups with field integration and R&D support.

Start-ups

The start-ups in the FAO–EBRD report work in smart and precision agriculture and related fields such as supply chain management and agricultural finance. The report's list of start-ups was taken from the 2022 Türkiye Agriculture Ecosystem map and completed with web research and meetings with about half of the start-ups; there is no regularly updated list of agricultural technology start-ups in Türkiye. FAO and the EBRD state that they do not endorse the companies named in the report. For the start-ups in the report that present an agricultural technology service on their own websites, the founding year and city come from the report; the descriptions of what they do are the companies' own statements:

The report lists Actimoo and BenimSürüm among the start-ups set up as subsidiaries of established companies. According to the report, Ankaref began R&D on a low-cost oestrus detection system for dairy cows with Ankara University and Algan Group in 2016 and launched a pedometer under the Actimoo brand in 2020; BenimSürüm's parent company is Algan Group. AgCurate was founded in Ankara; the address the company gives on its website is now in the Netherlands. Görsentam is the technology provider of the smart irrigation project in TÜBİTAK 1711.
