TARIM PORTALI · ALONG THE CHAIN

Advisory services and language models

https://tarimportali.net/yapay-zeka-dijital-tarim/danismanlik-ve-dil-modelleri

The effect of digital farm advice has been measured: according to meta-analyses, information delivered by mobile phone and digital channels raises yields by 4% and the odds of adopting recommended inputs by 22%. For language models the picture is mixed: in Nigeria, ChatGPT's answers to farmers' questions generally scored higher than those of extension agents, but fell behind them on the site- and time-specific matters farmers actually ask about, such as when to sow and how much fertiliser to apply. In Türkiye, the ministry has not yet announced a farmer-facing AI assistant or chatbot.

What it does

Agricultural extension is the service that carries research knowledge to farmers: which variety to sow, when and how much fertiliser to apply, how to recognise a disease. Digital technology takes this information by text message, voice call or mobile app to farmers that extension agents cannot reach. Large language models go a step further: they understand a farmer's question in the farmer's own words and generate an answer. Their risk comes from the same place: an answer can be fluent and still not know local conditions.

What the field says

A review by Fabregas, Kremer and Schilbach (2019) writes that the spread of mobile phones creates an opportunity to raise agricultural productivity sustainably for the 2 billion people living in smallholder farming households. According to the authors, the benefits of farm information delivered through digital channels likely exceed the cost of transmitting it by an order of magnitude. But messages designed by agricultural ministries are often hard for farmers to understand and use; the authors stress that feedback mechanisms are needed so the systems can be rigorously tested and continuously improved.

Individual trials show the differences behind the average. In a randomised trial in Nigeria of a mobile app giving personalised advice on fertilising rice (Arouna and colleagues, 2021), households that received the advice raised their yields by 7% and their profits by 10%, without increasing their total fertiliser use. In a two-year evaluation of voice-based phone advice for cotton farmers in India (Cole and Fernando, 2021), farmers changed their sources of information and consistently adopted the recommended inputs; but no systematic gain in yields or profitability was found.

The most direct comparison for language models also comes from Nigeria (Ibrahim and colleagues, 2024). 32 questions on irrigated rice collected from farmers in Kano State were answered by six randomly selected extension agents and by ChatGPT; four evaluators scored the answers for quality and local relevance. Overall, ChatGPT's answers scored significantly higher: they received the best score nearly six times as often as the agents' (40% against 7%), and the evaluators preferred ChatGPT in 78% of cases. But ChatGPT fell behind on planting time, seed rate, and fertiliser rate and timing. According to the authors, building site-specific input rates and timing into AI assistants is critical if farmers are to use them directly.

Where AI stands

The average effect of digital advice is small but positive and cheap. In the Nigerian trial, personalised advice raised yields and profits; according to the authors, blanket fertiliser advice had failed to achieve potential yield gains in much of Sub-Saharan Africa. The comparisons made for language models, for their part, measure the quality of answers; they do not measure the effect of language-model advice on farmers' yields or incomes.

Türkiye

According to the ministry's 2025 activity report, extension and advisory support payments went to 493 persons and organisations, 709 agricultural advisors and 36,831 farm holders. In farmer courses run with the Ministry of National Education, there were 1,123,252 farmer participations in 45,804 training and extension activities; the Agriculture and Forestry Academy, which runs remote training, has 100,086 registered users.

On the digital side, the ministry has 17 mobile apps. The Irrigation Management and Crop Water Use System developed by TAGEM (TAGEM-SuET) gives producers irrigation schedules in the "Su" (water) tab of the Tarım Cebimde app. The ministry says it is working on machine learning, deep learning and natural language processing as part of its AI activities. In the 2027 Programme, the only measure for a chatbot running on AI is for the tax administration. The Sectoral Turkish Large Language Model Call, under the responsibility of the Ministry of Industry and Technology, is to encourage adapting domestic language models to sectors. The Programme also aims to strengthen the link between agricultural research and extension. The effect of AI or language-model advice on farmers' yields, incomes or input use in Türkiye, however, has not yet been measured and published.

In academia, language models have begun to be tested on farm questions. A study from Karamanoğlu Mehmetbey University (Eldem and Eldem, 2026) found that, on a set of 90 four-option agricultural questions, GPT-4o's average accuracy ranged from 84.8% to 95.3% depending on the prompting strategy, and Gemini's from 74.8% to 88.4%; the lowest results came when the models were asked without examples (zero-shot). The questions were compiled from three online question banks (agricultureinindia.net, sanfoundry.com, examveda.com); the authors state that they made no comparison with fine-tuning, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) or traditional methods. No published study has tested language models with Turkish, open-ended, site-specific farmer questions. A study run in 96 tomato fields in Karacabey, Bursa (Şahin and colleagues, 2025) connected ChatGPT-4 to a model that recognises pests in images: the detection model reached 98.91% precision and 98.98% recall. But the success measured in the paper belongs to the detection model; there is no measurement of the accuracy of the explanations and recommendations given by the language model.
